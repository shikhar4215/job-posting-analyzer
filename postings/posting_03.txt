Job Title: Data Analyst
Company: Inspire11
Location: Multiple locations (onsite at office or client sites as needed)
Salary: $64,000 - $120,000 (Associate to Managing Consultant)

About the role:
Consulting analyst who bridges data and decision-making for clients, uncovering insights and designing data products.

Responsibilities:
- Gather client needs through stakeholder interviews, process mapping and exploratory analysis
- Assess raw data for trends, quality, performance metrics and business-logic accuracy
- Build dashboards and reports in Power BI or Tableau
- Work with cloud data platforms such as Fabric, Snowflake, Databricks, Redshift or Synapse
- Facilitate Agile ceremonies and co-creation workshops
- Explore AI and GenAI applications for automating insights and reporting

Requirements:
- 2+ years of consulting experience in data analysis, BI or analytics strategy
- Strong SQL skills for large datasets
- Hands-on experience with Power BI or Tableau
- Exposure to modern cloud data platforms
- Familiarity with AI/ML concepts and GenAI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Copilot)
- Strong communication, including presenting to external stakeholders
- Comfort with agile, cross-functional teams
