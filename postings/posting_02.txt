Job Title: Senior Data Analyst, Merchant Solutions
Company: Aperia Solutions
Location: Alpharetta, GA (on-site)
Salary: Not listed
Source: https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/aperiasolutions/jobs/5040038007

About the role:
Own the data, reporting, segmentation and statistical measurement behind SMB retention and engagement campaigns, supporting Product, Customer Support/Ops, Sales and Data Science.

Responsibilities:
- Own and maintain datasets for SMB retention and engagement initiatives
- Build data models for merchant cohorts, lifecycle stages, churn indicators and offer eligibility
- Create dashboards and weekly/monthly reporting for leadership and operations
- Define segmentation, eligibility and prioritization rules for campaigns
- Design test vs. control frameworks for campaigns
- Apply hypothesis testing, regression, uplift modeling and time-series analysis to measure impact
- Run deep dives on churn drivers, behavior, channel performance and campaign effectiveness

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in business, finance, analytics, engineering or related field, or equivalent experience
- 3+ years in data analytics, business intelligence or statistical analysis
- Strong SQL skills with large, complex, siloed datasets
- Proficiency with BI tools such as Looker, Tableau or Power BI
- Statistics: experimental design, A/B testing, hypothesis testing, regression, causal inference, confidence intervals
- Excellent communication and presentation skills

Preferred:
- Master's degree in data science, statistics, analytics, economics or related field
- Fintech, payments, SaaS or lifecycle/CRM experience
- Predictive modeling (churn, propensity, uplift)
- scikit-learn, statsmodels or R
- Marketing automation or CRM tools
