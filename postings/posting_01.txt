Job Title: Data Analyst
Company: LTK
Location: US-Remote
Salary: Not listed
Source: https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/shopltk/jobs/7671051003

About the role:
Support senior analysts and business leaders in Product, Finance and Operations with SQL analyses, dashboards and insights on revenue growth, retention and the creator/brand/shopper experience in a three-sided marketplace.

Responsibilities:
- Support analytics for specific product or business areas tied to revenue, retention, monetization and cost efficiency
- Help frame questions, define success metrics and turn business problems into analytical plans
- Produce recurring performance reports and ad hoc deep dives with recommendations
- Write SQL to pull, transform and validate warehouse data
- Build and maintain dashboards in Looker, Hex or Wisdom
- Help define and document core metrics; analyze experiments and A/B tests
- Automate repetitive reporting; follow analytics QA and dashboard best practices
- Use AI-assisted tools for analysis and summarization, validating outputs

Requirements:
- 2-4+ years in analytics, BI, data science or a similar quantitative role
- Strong SQL skills with large, complex datasets
- Experience with BI tools such as Looker, Hex, Wisdom or Tableau
- Strong Excel or Google Sheets skills
- Understanding of funnels, conversion and unit economics
- Clear written and verbal communication
- Bachelor's degree with a quantitative foundation (economics, statistics, engineering, math or computer science)

Nice to have:
- Python, R or similar tools for analysis or automation
