Job Title: Data Analyst I
Company: Lumos
Location: United States (FL, GA, NC, SC or VA)
Salary: Not listed
Source: https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/lumosfiber/jobs/4916602008

About the role:
Support data modeling and analysis at a fiber internet provider: mining, cleaning and visualizing data to produce reports, working with business analysts, product owners and leaders across care, marketing, product, sales, engineering and IT.

Responsibilities:
- Support data integrity and standardization
- Build reports from one or more systems
- Update existing reports; train users on new reports and dashboards
- Troubleshoot the reporting database and reports
- Evaluate changes to source production systems

Requirements:
- College degree or equivalent education and experience
- 2-3 years with large datasets and reporting tools such as Power BI, Tableau or Einstein Analytics
- Writing or updating SQL for data extraction and analysis
- Practical use of probability and statistical inference
- Proficiency in Excel, Word and PowerPoint, plus SQL and Power BI
- Ability to learn new platforms quickly

Nice to have:
- Snowflake and dbt
- Code repositories such as Bitbucket
