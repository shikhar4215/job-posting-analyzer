We have an exciting opportunity for a Data Analytics Intern to join our Meter Services team! 

This internship is a guided development opportunity for a student interested in data analytics and utility operations. Working closely with Meter Services mentors, the intern will receive coaching, regular feedback, and hands-on experience with real department projects. The succesful candidate will build practical skills in data cleanup, reporting, process documentation, and continuous improvement while learning how accurate information supports business decisions. The program is designed to strengthen professional confidence, technical ability, and career readiness.

At Citizens Energy Group, our Internship Program offers undergraduate students a welcoming and engaging opportunity to gain meaningful, hands-on experience while making a real impact. Interns work on projects that support our business and communities, while also participating in professional development activities, networking events, mentorship opportunities, and site visits designed to help them grow personally and professionally. While some positions may offer hybrid work flexibility, interns are encouraged to connect with peers and leaders through required in-person program events throughout the summer, creating a collaborative and memorable internship experience.
